Chess is more than a game. It is a journey of patience, imagination, calculation and decision-making. Our academy aims to make that journey engaging for learners at every stage.

  • Chess fundamentals for new learners
  • Strategic thinking and tactical practice
  • Learning paths for different skill levels
  • Chess puzzles and analytical exercises
  • Preparation and guidance for competitive play

Fundamentals

Know the pieces

Each piece moves differently. Learn how the king, queen, rook, bishop, knight and pawns work together.

Strategy

Control the centre

Central squares influence the activity and mobility of your pieces. Develop with purpose and coordinate your forces.

Explore chess strategy ↗
Tactics

Spot the fork

A fork is a double attack in which one piece attacks two or more enemy pieces at the same time.
